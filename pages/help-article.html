How to Upload Notes

Updated August 2026

Keep your revision material close to the rest of your Novexa study space.

1. Open Notes

Use the Notes link from the navbar or dashboard.

2. Click Upload

Select Upload note and choose a PDF, Word file or image.

3. Add a title

Add a clear title, subject and qualification so it is easy to find later.

4. Save

Your note stays attached to your account after you sign out.
